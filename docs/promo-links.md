## Tracking a specific post (optional)

Add a second tag to compare individual posts or subreddits:

- `https://brick-smasher-liart.vercel.app/?utm_source=reddit&utm_campaign=webgames` (r/WebGames)
- `https://brick-smasher-liart.vercel.app/?utm_source=reddit&utm_campaign=playmygame` (r/playmygame)
- `https://brick-smasher-liart.vercel.app/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_campaign=megapaddle-clip` (a specific video)

Keep tags lowercase, with no spaces (use hyphens).

## Notes

- Players' own shared score links (challenge links) work separately and don't need tags.
- `?fresh=1` is for testing only (it wipes that device's progress); never share it publicly.
